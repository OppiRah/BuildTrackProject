# BuildTrack Project Brief

Updated: 2026-10-04. Discovery settled; website implementation not started.

## Project and assignment
**BuildTrack — Smart Construction. Smarter Delivery.**
Proposed business: a smart construction materials and equipment management system for efficient project delivery services.

This is an individual Technopreneurship project owned and presented by a Civil Engineering student. A Computer Science student handles technical development with AI assistance. The professor has a Computer Engineering background. The result must be credible, public, functional and explainable to the presenter.

## Deliverable
A multi-page public construction-technology business website with a real demo/quotation/service inquiry flow and a polished interactive simulated product dashboard. It markets a proposed platform; it does not implement the production SaaS platform.

Visitors must understand: what BuildTrack is, the problem, the proposed solution, how it works, who it serves, services, proposed prices, Philippine coverage and how to request services. Required assignment content includes company background, branding/logo, products/services, prices, coverage and relevant photos/graphics.

## Problem and value
Construction teams have fragmented visibility over materials, inventory, deliveries, equipment, utilization and resource movements. BuildTrack proposes QR/RFID/IoT-enabled resource tracking feeding a cloud platform, dashboards, alerts and analytics.

Value proposition: **Know where your materials and equipment are, how they are being used, and what your project needs next.** Intended benefits include reduced waste/loss, improved inventory accuracy/utilization, better project visibility and fewer avoidable delays/costs. These are proposed benefits, not measured results of this prototype.

## Confirmed business decisions
- Philippines-based business and coverage. Overseas expansion may be described only as future vision, never current coverage.
- Preserve all BMC customer segments equally; do not downgrade government, developers or rental businesses to secondary customers.
- Proposed peso pricing including Enterprise numeric pricing. Amounts approved 2026-10-04 (ADR-025).
- Friend will supply/input his approved location and contact number.
- Availing means submitting a request, quotation inquiry or demo request. No monetary checkout.
- The professor must be able to submit a real request that reaches the designated contact.

## Customer segments
Construction companies (small, medium, large); contractors/subcontractors; project managers/site engineers; civil engineering firms; government infrastructure projects; real estate developers; equipment rental companies. Suppliers and technology providers remain partners unless a later approved decision changes that classification. The professor is the evaluator, not a separate business customer segment.

## Business Model Canvas
- Partners: material suppliers, equipment rental companies, QR/RFID/IoT providers, cloud providers, software partners, engineering/construction firms, government agencies/projects.
- Activities: develop/maintain the proposed platform; tracking/inventory; data analysis/reporting; integration; onboarding/training/support; updates; marketing/partnerships.
- Resources: proposed platform/mobile app, cloud/database systems, tracking hardware, developers/engineers, analytics tools and brand/IP.
- Relationships: support, training/onboarding, tutorials, updates, contracts, feedback and account management.
- Channels: website; proposed mobile app; social media; email; direct sales/partnerships; expos/trade fairs; webinars/demos.
- Revenue: subscriptions/licensing, premium analytics, hardware, training/implementation, custom integration/consulting, maintenance/support.
- Costs: development/cloud/servers, salaries, hardware, marketing, implementation/training, support, maintenance, R&D and administration.
These describe the proposed business, not systems or commercial relationships already implemented.

## Actual versus simulated
Actual: deployed website, navigation, plan/service preselection, form validation/submission and verified delivery.
Simulated: construction projects, inventory, equipment, deliveries, tracking events, device states and analytics.
Concept only: operational SaaS, hardware tracking, mobile app and production integrations. Use visible prototype/demo disclosures; do not pretend a real operational company deployment exists.

## Success and knowledge transfer
The professor can browse the public HTTPS site on desktop/mobile, understand the business, inspect prices/coverage, explore the labeled demo and submit a request the contact receives. The friend can explain the business, pages, request flow, simulated data, frontend/form-service distinction and basic hosting/domain concepts. A presentation guide is written after the actual site exists; framework internals and memorizing source code are unnecessary.
